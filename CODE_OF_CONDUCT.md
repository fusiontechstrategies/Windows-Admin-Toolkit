# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and productive open-source community for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about security and operational risk.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials, private infrastructure details, or confidential data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Deliberate disclosure of private information
- Promotion of unauthorized access, destructive activity, or misuse of the toolkit
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, discussions, or reviews

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
